## Project-specific setup and exclusions

Only a generic Card Atlas source shell is published. The publisher-derived catalog, download/import script, publisher-specific fixture tests, original icon, catalog manifest and local verification records are excluded. `App/Resources/cards.json` is deliberately an empty JSON array. Supply your own independently licensed data and artwork; catalog search and OCR identification remain empty until then. Historical project/target filenames remain for source compatibility. This snapshot grants no publisher license and is not a complete playable commercial-game adaptation.
